# Asthra Vision

Asthra is an AI-native operating platform for modern teams. It is designed to bring work, engineering, knowledge, service, reliability, collaboration, automation, governance, and intelligence into a coherent platform.

The platform direction is modular and staged. Asthra starts with a strong foundation, then grows into suite capabilities that can share identity, permissions, workspace context, and operational history.

The first milestone is not feature breadth. The first milestone is a clean structure, clear service boundaries, durable documentation, and engineering standards that can support long-term platform development.
