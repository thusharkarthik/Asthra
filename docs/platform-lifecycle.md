# Platform Lifecycle

Asthra connects the execution lifecycle from opportunity to measurable outcome.

```text
Discover
    ↓
Docs
    ↓
Flow
    ↓
Dev
    ↓
Desk
    ↓
Pulse
    ↓
Insights
```

## Lifecycle Meaning

Everything begins as an opportunity.

Opportunities become:

- Ideas
- Knowledge
- Work
- Releases
- Operations
- Metrics
- Reports

## Stage Responsibilities

Discover captures and validates opportunities.

Docs turns opportunities and decisions into durable knowledge.

Flow turns knowledge into planned and executable work.

Dev connects execution to engineering delivery.

Desk manages service requests and operational support.

Pulse monitors health, incidents, capacity, and objectives.

Insights turns operational data into dashboards and reports.

## Lifecycle Rule

Each lifecycle stage owns its data. Related records should be connected through APIs, events, or relationship models rather than duplicated across services.
