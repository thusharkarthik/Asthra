## Task: Profile Page Bug Fix (stale form after save)

Root cause: `useEffect` deps `[profileQuery.data, currentUser]` — stale `profileQuery.data` (cached) took precedence over updated auth store after PATCH /me succeeded.

Fix: In `onSuccess`, call `queryClient.setQueryData(["me", "profile"], updatedUser)` to synchronously update cache before useEffect re-runs. Also update form state directly + update auth store.
