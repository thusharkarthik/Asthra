## Fix 2 — Member List Cache Invalidation (Frontend)

### What changed

**`frontend/src/components/settings/settings-admin-views.tsx`**:
- Added `["settings", "members"]` and `["settings", "global-member-role-assignments"]` to `invalidateSettingsAndContext()`.

**`frontend/src/hooks/use-settings-mutations.ts`**:
- Added the same two invalidation keys to `useInviteMemberMutation.onSuccess` and `useAssignRoleMutation.onSuccess`.

### Root cause
`invalidateSettingsAndContext` invalidated `queryKeys.members.all` = `["members"]`. TanStack Query prefix-matches this — it matched `["members", "list", ...]` (global directory) but NOT `["settings", "members", orgId, wsId]` (scoped member list) because that key lives in the `"settings"` namespace.
